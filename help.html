01 · Start

What the Auto-Sorter does

You have a Figma file (or five) full of email designs. The Auto-Sorter reads that file and lays every email out on one canvas, already grouped. You can see it by brand, by type (campaign or flow), by category (Black Friday, launches, social proof and more), by the section it’s built around, and by language, year and length.

Click any card to see the full design. One more click opens that exact frame in Figma. Nothing gets copied or moved: the sorter is a map of your file, and Figma stays the source.

It’s app Nº 01 of the Hall of Templates Studio Apps, the tools we use in our own email design studio.

02 · Start

Sort a file in 3 steps

  1. Copy your file link. Open the Figma file with your email designs and copy the link from the browser bar (or Share → Copy link).
  2. Make a Figma token. In Figma: your name (top left) → Settings → Security → Personal access tokens → Generate new token. Name it “Auto-Sorter”, set File content to Read-only, leave everything else off, create it and copy it. Figma shows it only once. More on tokens
  3. Paste both and click Sort my library. A big file takes up to a minute the first time. After that the library opens instantly in the same browser.
Added new designs in Figma? Open Files (top left of the canvas) → Re-sort from Figma.

03 · Start

Your Figma token, explained

A personal access token is how Figma lets an app read a file for you without your password. The sorter needs one permission only, File content: Read-only. With it, the sorter can see layer names and sizes and ask Figma for preview images. It can’t edit, comment, delete or see anything else.

Where it lives

The token is saved in this browser only. With “Remember on this device” on, it stays until you remove it. With it off, it’s forgotten when you close the tab. The page only sends it to api.figma.com; a browser rule built into the page blocks every other address.

Turn it off any time

In Figma, Settings → Security → Personal access tokens, click Revoke next to “Auto-Sorter”. In the sorter, Files → Forget token and files removes it from the browser.

Tokens can expire (Figma lets you pick 30 or 90 days, or no expiry). When one does, the sorter asks for a new one. Your sorted library stays.

04 · Start

Privacy and security

  • No server. The sorter is a static web page. Your browser talks to Figma directly; nothing passes through us.
  • No account, no tracking. We don’t see your files, your token, your designs or how you use the app.
  • Read-only. It never writes to your Figma file.
  • Stored locally. Your sorted library, thumbnails and Pro unlock are saved in this browser’s storage. Clearing site data removes them.

Client files are safe to sort. Just keep in mind that anyone using your computer and browser can open the library you sorted there.

05 · Your file

What counts as an email

The sorter treats a frame, component, instance or group as an email when it’s 320 to 900px wide and at least three quarters as tall as it is wide. That covers 600px email designs, mobile previews and long newsletters. Banners, icons and loose sections are ignored.

Emailify building blocks (🚣 rows and 🎁 wrappers) are never counted as emails, even when they’re big.

It looks inside pages, Figma Sections and frames, including sections nested a few levels deep.

06 · Your file

Naming that sorts perfectly

The sorter reads names; it doesn’t guess from pixels. A few habits make every group land right:

GroupWhat it readsDo this
BrandThe Figma Section the email sits inOne Section per brand: “Acme Co”, “Bluleaf”
TypeFlow words in the frame name“Welcome Series: Email #2”, “Abandoned Cart 1”, “Win-back”. Everything else is a campaign.
CategoryKeywords in the name, then the layers“Black Friday early access”, “New in: Summer drop”, “Reviews roundup”
SectionNames of the email’s top-level layersName blocks Hero, Product grid, Reviews, Discount code, Footer
LanguageTags like (EN) or NL |“(FR) Spring sale”
YearDates in the name250514, “May 14, 2025”, 14.05.25
LengthFrame heightNothing to do: under 1600px is short, under 3000px medium

Categories understand English, Spanish and Dutch keywords. When a name says nothing, the layers decide: a Reviews block makes it social proof, a Discount code block makes it a sale.

07 · Your file

The starter file

Starting from scratch, or want to see the rules in action? Open the starter file, click the file name → Duplicate, and replace the examples with your designs.

It has a Read me page, an Emails page with two brand Sections and six example emails, and a Parts page with atoms, molecules and organisms as components. Everything in it is named the way the sorter reads, so it sorts 6 emails and 23 parts with no guessing.

08 · The canvas

Moving around

  • Drag to move. ⌘ or Ctrl + scroll (or pinch) to zoom. Plain scrolling pans.
  • Hover a card to light up its closest relatives in the same group.
  • Click a card for the full-size design, its tags and Open in Figma. Arrow keys step through cards.
  • Chips above the dock fly you to a group. Fit shows everything. Surprise me picks a design you haven’t opened yet.
  • The Discovered meter counts the designs you’ve opened, so you can tell what you’ve already reviewed.
  • Search filters by name as you type.
Previews appear once you’re zoomed in enough to read a card, so zoomed-out views stay fast.

09 · The canvas

Groups and filters

The dock at the bottom regroups the whole canvas: All, Brand, Type, Category, Section, Language, Year, Length and, with several files in Pro, File.

Filter (top right) narrows the canvas to what you need right now, for example only Black Friday campaigns with a product grid, in Spanish. Filters combine with the grouping and the search.

11 · Pro

The parts library

Switch the top toggle from Emails to Parts to see the pieces your emails are made of, the way the Atomic Emails method names them:

  • Atoms (elements): buttons, badges, logos, social icons.
  • Molecules (containers): a product card, an image with text, a review card.
  • Organisms (sections): a hero, a product grid, a footer.

Parts come from two places: a page in your file named Parts (with sections named Atoms, Molecules and Organisms), and parts the sorter finds by atomizing your emails. Group them by level, type, brand, how many emails use them, or where they came from.

12 · Pro

Atomize emails

In Parts mode, Atomize emails opens every email in your files and breaks it into organisms, molecules and atoms. Parts that are identical across emails (same name, size, structure and text) collapse into one, with a count: “used in 34 emails”. That count shows you which sections and buttons your whole library is really built from.

  • It’s read-only. Nothing is written to Figma.
  • It takes a few minutes on a big file. Our three 1,000-template files (832 emails) took about 8: 17,876 parts, 8,227 unique.
  • Results are kept in this browser. Run it again after big changes.
Atomize reads Emailify structure directly: 🚣 rows become organisms, 🏛️ columns become molecules, 🔗 buttons and logos become atoms. Files built another way use the email’s full-width blocks as organisms.

13 · Pro

Turn parts into real Figma components

The sorter shows you your parts. To make them reusable components inside Figma, use the atomic-extractor skill for Claude. It’s included in Atomic Emails, our core workshop. Claude dry-runs first, then builds a Parts page in your file: one component per part, grouped by type, ranked by reuse. The sorter reads that page in Parts mode.

On a real client file it turned 392 emails into a 240-component library in two runs.

14 · Help

Troubleshooting

“Figma says this token is not valid”

The token was revoked, expired or copied with a space. Make a new one (File content: Read-only) and paste it with Files → Change token.

“No access to this file”

The token belongs to a Figma account that can’t open that file. Open the link in Figma with the same account. If it’s a client’s file, ask for view access.

“No email templates found”

Nothing in the file matches the email size rule. Check that the designs are frames (not one giant image) and 320 to 900px wide.

Previews load slowly

Figma renders each preview on its own servers, and image-heavy emails can take a few seconds each. The counter at the top left shows how many are left. Once rendered, previews are cached for 20 days.

“Figma asked us to slow down”

Figma limits how many requests a token makes per minute. The sorter waits and carries on by itself; leave the tab open.

My library or Pro disappeared

Browser data was cleared, or you’re on another browser or computer. Sort again and paste your Pro key once more.

15 · Help

Questions

Do I need Emailify?

No. Any Figma email design works. Emailify names make Atomize extra precise.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Each person uses their own Figma token on their own computer. Pro is personal: one key per buyer.

Does it work on a phone?

It opens on a phone or tablet, but sorting and browsing a big library is best on a laptop.

Refunds?

If Pro doesn’t sort your file, email us within 30 days with the error you see. We’ll fix it, or refund you, no questions.
